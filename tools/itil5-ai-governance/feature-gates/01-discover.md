# Activity 1: Discover — Minimum Guaranteed Features
> **ITIL 5 Lifecycle Activity 1 of 8** | 6C Capabilities: Clarification, Cognition
> Based on ITIL 5 preview spec (PeopleCert, 2026); will align to final release.

The Discover activity establishes the foundational understanding required before any AI system investment proceeds. It surfaces the problem space, maps affected stakeholders, and determines the regulatory envelope that will constrain all downstream activities. Skipping or shortcutting Discover creates technical debt that compounds through Design and Build. All outputs from this activity feed directly into the ITIL 5 Value Chain entry gate.

| Feature | Success Criteria | Evidence File | Status |
|---------|-----------------|---------------|--------|
| Use Case Definition | AI purpose is documented with measurable business outcome; no ambiguity in scope | docs/use-case.md | ☐ |
| Stakeholder Mapping | RACI matrix completed; all impacted roles identified including end-users and regulators | docs/raci.md | ☐ |
| Risk Classification | EU AI Act risk tier assigned (Unacceptable / High / Limited / Minimal); justification recorded | docs/risk-tier.md | ☐ |
| Data Inventory | All training and inference data sources listed with owner, sensitivity label, and refresh cadence | docs/data-sources.md | ☐ |
| Regulatory Scope | JP, US, and EU applicability assessed; gaps between jurisdictions documented | docs/regulatory-scope.md | ☐ |
| Success Metrics | KPIs defined with pre-AI baseline values; measurement method and owner assigned | docs/kpis.md | ☐ |
